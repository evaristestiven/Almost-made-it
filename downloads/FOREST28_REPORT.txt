ALMOST MADE IT! — FOREST-28
Verified pine integration, retaining the ADVENTURE-27 gameplay upgrade

ASSETS USED
Pine_1.gltf + Pine_1.bin (3947 triangles)
Pine_5.gltf + Pine_5.bin (1646 triangles)
Leaf_Pine_C.png: original 2048x2048 RGBA source preserved; imported at 1024px
with mipmaps to reduce texture memory and distant aliasing.
Bark_NormalTree.png and Bark_NormalTree_Normal.png: verified existing shared
maps reused. Filenames, geometry bytes, UVs and glTF texture references unchanged.
The two pines were copied into assets/nature/quaternius/CommonTree_3/ alongside
the already-present bark maps. No entire-pack import or duplicate bark textures.

PLACEMENT AND RENDERING
Level1's outer forest now mixes 92 Pine_1 and 100 Pine_5 instances with 302
CommonTree_3 and 152 CommonTree_4 instances. Original detailed entrance trees
are preserved. Pine roots follow the actual decorative terrain, and branch
width is scaled for the existing groves. All use the same regional MultiMesh
batching and distance culling as the prior broadleaf forest, without colliders.
Four GL Compatibility captures were inspected: both isolated models, bark/leaf
close-up, Starting Garden and Windmill Hills. Bark, normal maps, leaf atlas and
alpha masking render correctly. A separate two-pine test scene is included.

GAMEPLAY UPGRADE RETAINED
Three raised stepping-stone links, two optional raised coin gardens, alternating
courtyard approaches and two rising gorge ferries remain from ADVENTURE-27.
No shared player/input/camera/physics or course configuration changed in this
pine pass. Level1 still has 5 coins, 4 checkpoints and a continuous five-area route.
The full 60Hz route, with both optional coin gardens, completed in 165.08 simulated
seconds, 0 falls, 5 coins and one victory. These are scripted test times, not
human first-clear or on-device performance measurements. ADVENTURE-27 previously
passed full traversal at 30/60/120Hz in169.47/165.08/164.48s; those physics settings
and course definitions are unchanged here. Skipping the optional gardens still
permits completion; coins are not required to win.

LIMITATIONS
This improves species variety toward Preview_2; it is not the complete preview
forest or a claim of photorealism. Evergreen foliage is alpha-masked leaf geometry,
not individual simulated needles. New pines have no physics or CPU wind simulation.
Existing terrain faceting, sky banding, simpler flowers and buildings remain.
iPhone/iPad FPS, thermal behavior, real touch feel and all possible camera/tree
occlusion cases remain unverified. No production-readiness claim is made.
