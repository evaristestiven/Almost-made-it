IMPLEMENTED
1 Three raised, offset stepping-stone connections replace straight bridge spans:
  Starting Garden cell4, Training Courtyard cell18 and Finish Plaza cell54. Each
  has a real supported landing; players can chain short precision jumps or try
  the longer direct jump where their skill permits. No movement speed changes.
2 Two optional raised coin gardens branch from garden cell7 and hills cell43.
  Five total coins remain. The main route remains completable without these two
  coins. Gold ground arrows and small labels signal the alcoves. Flowers stay
  behind the pickup and the center of the landing stays clear.

  existing deterministic angular speeds and solid collision implementation are
  preserved. Waiting/recovery courtyards remain; there is no random timing.

  Their visible meshes and enabled solid shapes move together through native
  AnimatableBody3D platform physics. All 13 route ferries remain in use.
5 Brighter soft grass and warmer dirt paths, subtle foliage color/backlighting,
  additional grounded imported tree/plant clusters and below-edge rock details
  move the scenery toward Preview_2's green forest palette. The existing windmills,
  waterfalls, houses, five districts and crown finish remain.
