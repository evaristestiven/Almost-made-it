Open project.godot and Run Project. This separate package starts the prototype.
In the original project, open prototypes/training_courtyard/training_courtyard.tscn
and Run Current Scene. The original project startup and playable Level1 are unchanged.

Drag to move, JUMP to clear sweeps. Arrow keys and Space are available on desktop.
RESET restarts this prototype only. PAUSE/RESUME clears input safely.
VIEW cycles overview, entrance, mechanism, exit and normal gameplay cameras.
Desktop: V cycles views, R resets, P pauses. Return to normal view before moving.

Built: circular paved stone/metal arena, golden inlays, compass marks, rivets,
custom turned mechanical hub, four substantial red enamel sweep arms with
ivory bands, tiered tower roofs, timber braces, banners, wooden bridges with
individual planks/rails/fasteners, landscaped satellite terraces, sculpted
cliff undersides and distant islands. Warm directional sunlight, limited
shadows, atmospheric haze and the existing animated layered-cloud sky.
Architecture is custom programmatic modular geometry, not imported art.
Rounded arms use two continuous perpendicular centered box colliders;
visual bevels are 0.04m, band trim overhang is 0.004m. Collision is conservative
at small beveled corners rather than a falsely claimed exact rounded hull.

Imported verified Quaternius assets reused without reimporting the whole pack:
CommonTree_3/4/5, Pine_1/5, Rock_Medium_3, Plant_1 and their existing textures.
Original files and texture relationships are unchanged. Decorative flowers
are modest custom five-petal meshes; no professional flower/bush/grass model
is present in the verified set. Plant_1 provides the leafy ground-cover clumps.
Missing: authored courtyard architecture/props, authored cloth and flower/grass
varieties, completed TwistedTree bark and leaf textures. No paid asset assumed.

Physics: original player.gd, touch_controls.gd, follow_camera.gd and spinner.gd
are reused unchanged. Fixed-step AnimatableBody3D native solids plus the existing
angular sweep/separation queries and v_contact = omega cross r feed the original
relative-velocity, effective mass, restitution and friction response.
Protection blocks hit impulses temporarily while preserving solid collision.
Safe outside ring supports waiting/dodging; jump clearance is physically tested.
Initial setup defect found and repaired only in prototype: position the animated
body before adding it to the tree, so physics synchronization retains its arena
location. Explicit local reset toggles sync while resetting the mechanism pose.

Optimization: 123 grouped MultiMesh batches / 2612 modular instances initially;
seven imported mesh variants, regional vegetation groups, visibility culling,
reused materials, limited shadow distance and no vegetation physics. The extra
architecture is not a device FPS guarantee. No volumetric clouds or SSAO.
