BUILT
A 24.6m playable circular arena with individually spaced stone paving sectors,
metal inlay rings, compass marks and rivets. A custom turned mechanical hub
supports four substantial red enamel sweep arms with small rounded bevels,
ivory bands, brass trim and hub fasteners. A safe outer ring provides waiting
and dodge space. Entry and exit wooden bridges are solid, continuously connected
and have individual planks, fasteners, rail posts, caps and rails.
Four large decorative towers and smaller gateway towers use modular bases,
shafts, windows, timber braces, tiered roofs, finials and animated banners.
Landscaped islands frame the arena, with sculpted cliff undersides, imported
tree clusters, rocks and leafy ground cover. Distant island groups add depth.
Warm sunlight, restrained shadows, haze and a new angular cloud panorama
provide a cheerful sky. Cloud cubemap parameters update only twice per second.

ASSETS USED
Existing verified Quaternius CommonTree_3/4/5, Pine_1/5, Rock_Medium_3 and Plant_1.
Their original geometry buffers, material textures and filenames are unchanged.
No whole-pack import, new paid asset or missing-texture substitution.
Plant_1 supplies leafy clumps, not a falsely claimed imported grass species.
Small custom five-petal flowers supplement the available vegetation.

NEW GEOMETRY / MATERIALS
The arena, towers, hub, arms, bridges, cliffs, rails and flowers are original
programmatic modular geometry. They are not professional imported building
models. Material variations include limestone, warm paving, enamel, dark metal,
brass, timber, meadow surfaces and cloth. Materials use corrected linear colors
and controlled lighting rather than excessive bloom. Flags reuse the existing
cloth shader; the prototype sky shader avoids the former stretched horizon.

PHYSICS
Original player.gd, spinner.gd, touch_controls.gd and follow_camera.gd are
reused unchanged. The new sweep script subclasses the existing spinner and
adds two centered perpendicular solid boxes, forming four continuous arms.
Native fixed-step AnimatableBody3D motion and the existing angular shape sweep
resolve solid displacement. Contact velocity omega cross r, relative velocity,
effective mass, restitution and friction feed the existing external-velocity
channel. Motor release does not zero knockback. Cooldown/protection suppress
extra hit impulses while retaining solid collision.
Visual arm edge bevel is0.04m; band trim projects0.004m. Box collision is slightly
conservative at the small beveled corners; it is not an exact rounded hull.

CONFIRMED DEFECTS CAUGHT AND REPAIRED IN THIS PROTOTYPE
1. The sweep initially synchronized back to origin because position was set
after adding its body. Position is now set before entering the scene tree.
2. World-axis scaling distorted diagonal rails and gateway crossbeams.
Local-axis scaling now preserves full spans and endpoints.
3. Initial color/tonemap/fog settings washed out the scene and hid the sky.
Corrected linear colors, exposure, sunlight and sky fog restored contrast.
4. Inspection cameras intersected a tower/gateway. Final views are reframed.
5. Decorative rocks intruded onto the outer arena. Foundation rocks were
lowered, and imported rock bounds are excluded from the playable annulus.
No shared gameplay scripts were changed to repair these prototype defects.

REFERENCE COMPARISON / LIMITATIONS
The central circular arena, red four-arm mechanism, bridge approaches, towers,
banners, imported foliage and clear hazard silhouettes follow the blueprint.
It remains visibly simpler than the reference: tower/cliff silhouettes are
modular, terrain caps are comparatively flat, paving/material detail is less
authored, ground vegetation lacks several species, and the distant skyline
has substantial open sky. Clouds are inexpensive2D shading, not volumetric.
Arms are beveled beams rather than fully cylindrical padded sweep arms.
The entrance view intentionally includes the gate crossbeam at the lower edge.
This is a functional visual prototype, not established reference parity or a
claim that the whole game's visual standard is finished.

MISSING ASSETS / PRODUCTION NEEDS
More detailed authored courtyard stonework, roof/trim modules, hub/arm skins,
terrain transition meshes, flower/bush/grass variants and richer material maps
would improve production art. Available trees are preserved, not replaced with
primitive sphere trees. TwistedTree models still require Bark_TwistedTree.png,
Bark_TwistedTree_Normal.png and Leaves_TwistedTree_C.png and were excluded.

MOBILE / READINESS
The final scene uses123 grouped MultiMesh batches and2520 modular instances,
reuses materials, culls distant scenery, limits shadow distance and adds no
vegetation physics. Shadow settings and transparent foliage still need device
profiling. iPhone/iPad Xogot frame pacing, touch feel, thermals and all possible
camera/scenery occlusion cases were not tested. No production-readiness claim.
Use this isolated build for visual and mobile review before integrating it
into Level1. No other course areas have been built in this phase.
