ALMOST MADE IT! — WORLD-25
LEVEL 1 IMMERSIVE ENVIRONMENT INTEGRATION

BUILT IN THE EXISTING PLAYABLE LEVEL
The five areas remain one continuous course. This is a visual pass over the
existing 60 landings, 855m route, 4 checkpoints and 5 coins. No course rebuild.

Starting Garden: imported stone path accents, flower-bed clusters, butterflies,
section flags, varied side hills and existing detailed Quaternius tree groves.
Training Courtyard: three decorative machine installations with six turning,
meshed gears, purple/gold cloth banners and a textured stone training surface.
Waterfall Crossing: layered imported rock clusters, a flowing teal/foam shader,
eight inexpensive mist cards and wood-toned bridge/recovery deck materials.
Windmill Hills: preserved three animated windmills with scenic fence/bench
arrangements, small houses, flower beds and varied wooded side islands.
Finish Plaza: flanking gardens and banners, a continuous curved golden arch,
custom extruded crown with three colored jewels and a distant island backdrop.
The finish trigger and its collision geometry have not moved.

COHESION AND DEPTH
Near detail uses existing clumps, flowers and rocks. Middle-distance landmarks
identify each district. Ten district side islands and three finish-backdrop
islands extend the wooded landscape. Trees are the existing imported models,
with terrain-supported roots. Materials share warm/green/teal/purple accents.
Sky uses inexpensive layered noise clouds, blue atmospheric gradient and haze.
Warm sunlight is preserved with modest ambient/exposure/shadow-blur adjustments.
No additional lights, volumetric clouds, physics decorations or particle systems.

MOBILE BUDGET AND LIMITS
Repeated vegetation/rocks/pavers use existing regionally culled MultiMeshes.
Shaders/materials are shared. Details disappear at 60–125m; existing distant
landscape uses longer visibility ranges. Only six new gears need CPU rotation,
and only while near the camera; cloth/water/mist motion is shader-driven.
Sky radiance uses 128px normally/64px low quality. No new large textures.
Measured draws/primitives are in render logs; these do not establish mobile FPS.
Use the existing visuals/sky_garden/quality setting (0 low, 1 balanced, 2 high).

REMAINING LIMITATIONS
This is a targeted enhancement, not an exact reproduction of the concept art.
Cliffs and distant terrain retain some visibly faceted/repeated geometry.
Clouds are noise-based, with some visible gradient banding on software OpenGL.
Some structures and flowers remain simple stylized custom meshes. Terrain and
shadow quality need an iPhone/iPad review. No device performance, touch testing,
thermal/power measurements or direct Xogot testing were possible here.
No production-readiness or guaranteed device-FPS claim is made.
