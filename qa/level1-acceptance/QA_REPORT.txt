CONFIRMED BUGS / REPAIRS
No reproducible gameplay defect was found in this acceptance pass. No gameplay,
physics, scenery, difficulty or level-layout changes were necessary.
The new QA harness initially made three incorrect assumptions: nonzero vertical
travel identified only the shutter (two diagonal ferries also rise slightly),
net displacement detected all ferry travel (turnarounds can return near the start),
and a process/physics observation occurred once per physics tick at 120Hz.
Those harness issues were corrected: classify the vertical-only shutter, measure
ferry path length, and check actual physics-frame duration independently of
camera sample count. All affected suites were rerun at all three rates. Initial
failed diagnostic logs are retained with fixture-error names; they are not
reported as passing game tests.
